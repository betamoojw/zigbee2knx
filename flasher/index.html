zigbee2knx

Zigbee devices as KNX devices, with one gateway that is the Zigbee coordinator itself.

Install from the browser. Chrome, Edge or Opera on a PC. Plug the gateway (TUL32, ESP32-C6 with an NCN5130) into USB and press the button.

This browser cannot flash from a web page; use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a PC. Flashing needs a page served over HTTPS.

First installation: let the installer erase the device. An update: do not erase; then the Zigbee network and the KNX devices stay and need not be paired again.

The TULX32 has no USB port for this; it installs firmware from its own recovery system.

Product files, getting started, the source and the licence (GPL-3.0): github.com/tostmann/zigbee2knx.
